# Decision inbox

Implement the user's email-app reference as CAR's real decision workflow, not an
email client or a separate prototype.

## Direction

- A quiet left sidebar for Needs you, Watching, Handled, and Settings.
- A compact source / subject / preview / time list, with a clearly selected row.
- One reading pane: context, source recommendation, uncertainty, and a reply.
- Neutral white and gray surfaces, restrained type, thin dividers, minimal badges.
  Dark mode follows the same hierarchy, without neon accents or colored card stacks.
- Exact answers and consequences remain visible before choosing. Technical
  provenance and withdrawal controls live in secondary disclosures.
- Desktop keeps the list and reader together. Small screens move between list and
  message with a clear back link; neither layout depends on JavaScript.

## Acceptance

- Preserve core state, counts, pagination, permissions, revision checks, and drafts.
- Guided requests, native questions, missed deadlines, and delivery problems remain
  reachable. Recording, receipt, and resolution remain visibly distinct.
- Check the actual rendered interface against the reference, plus narrow-screen,
  dark-mode, empty, selected, and answer states.
- Leave the existing local workspace and its answers intact and ready to test.
